# COST — Buffett-style business memo

**Verdict: own the business.**

Costco cleared the three business-quality checks. ROE was at least 15% in every displayed fiscal year, ranging from 25.1% to 31.2%. Owner earnings were positive in every year and rose from $3,501 million at 2022-08-28 to $9,382 million at 2026-08-30. Net income also rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million.

The record shows a business that converts its operations into cash while earning consistently strong returns on year-end equity. Capital spending increased from $3,891 million to $6,435 million, yet owner earnings still increased. On these operating facts alone, it looks like a business an owner could be pleased to hold and watch.

This is a judgment about the business, not its price. No market-price data is considered here.

Decision: I trust this verdict as a limited business-quality screen because every cited figure comes from a 10-K fact selected and calculated by code; a real investment decision would still require a price and deeper qualitative work.
